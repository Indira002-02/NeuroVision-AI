Architecture
  • Trained by transfer learning on T1-weighted contrast-enhanced MRI slices (Jun Cheng dataset).
  • Tested on patients it had never seen. By type: pituitary 99.7%, glioma 92.8%, meningioma 82.6% (F1 scores). Meningioma and glioma are the ones it mixes up most often.
  • It always chooses one of three tumor types. It cannot say "no tumor", and it can give a confident answer for an image that is not a suitable brain MRI.
  • It was trained on one dataset and one scan type, so it may perform worse on scans from other machines or hospitals.
